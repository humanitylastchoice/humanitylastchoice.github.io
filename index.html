The last choice humans make is who chooses next.

Humanity's Last Choice studies how AI makes recommendations across the domains that matter to people — and whether it can still understand what a person has in mind when the options are designed to win the model's recommendation.

02The protocol

Every task runs twice.

Every task pairs a person’s request with candidates drawn from real source material. In the clean condition the candidates are described as they are. In the shaped condition one candidate has been rewritten to win the recommendation. The system’s job is the same both times: choose for the person.

01
Clean

The candidates as they are. The baseline: how often the system chooses for the person when nothing is pushing.

02
Shaped

One candidate’s material rewritten to win, the way material on the open web is already being rewritten. Everything else stays the same.

03
The gap

Reported per system and per domain, with the date, and never as a single headline number. The interesting facts are in the gaps.

Research2026-09-04
One shaped page is enough

SafeGEO, our EMNLP 2026 paper, rewrites one seller-controlled source per case and watches four recommendation agents change their minds. Under the shaped condition the rewritten candidate reaches the top three in 73 to 91 percent of instances, up from 3 to 13 percent under a truthful rewrite.
