A shared question.

Choosing an AI system increasingly means delegating the choices that follow. We study whether those recommendations still serve the person when the sources a system reads are written to influence it. Our work grows through papers, shared evaluations, and contributions across the kinds of choice people delegate.

Our benchmark.

The Humanity’s Last Choice benchmark measures how AI recommendations change when the sources describing the options are written to influence the system. It grows across the kinds of choice people delegate, with each domain documenting its tasks, conditions, and results.
